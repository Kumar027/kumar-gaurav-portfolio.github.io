Marketing Campaign Performance Analysis
Multi-channel campaign performance, audience segmentation & funnel analysis

Build and deliver a Power BI dashboard to measure multi-channel performance, find high-value audiences, diagnose funnel issues, and recommend steps to improve ROI.

📄 BRD (docx) 📊 Power BI (pbix)
Project Objective

Build a Power BI dashboard to evaluate multi-channel marketing performance, identify high-value audience segments, uncover funnel issues, and provide data-driven recommendations to improve ROI and campaign efficiency.

Key Contributions
  • Designed and delivered a Power BI dashboard with dynamic filters (channel, segment, audience, geography).
  • Created a structured Business Requirements Document (BRD) defining objectives, KPIs and business questions.
  • Transformed raw marketing data (2 years) into actionable insights for performance improvement.
  • Built custom KPIs: CPC, CPL, Leads, Conversion Rate, ROI, Engagement Score.
  • Conducted deep analysis of segments, channels, demographics and customer behaviour.
Major Insights
  • Strong traffic, but funnel inefficiency causes low conversion and high CPL.
  • Best converting groups: Men 25–34, Women 25–34, Tech Enthusiasts, Health & Wellness.
  • Instagram, Google Ads and Website show consistent channel strength.
  • Men 18–24 produce many clicks but have poor conversion intent.
  • Lifestyle segments convert better with visual content; high-intent segments prefer information-rich messaging.
Recommendations
  • Prioritise Instagram & Facebook for lifestyle segments; use visuals, reels, and creators.
  • Use Google Ads & Email for high-intent groups with long-form, problem-solving content.
  • Build segment-specific landing pages; reduce form friction and sharpen CTAs.
  • Increase budget toward Instagram, Google Ads and Website.
  • Run A/B tests on CTAs and messaging; strengthen retargeting to reduce CPL.
